When something costs me time on every release, I turn it into a tool, test it on my own Steam games and share it.

Easy Ship Kit for Steam

A Unity editor tool: build, check and upload your game to Steam from one window.

  • Build profiles — Release, Test, Demo — each with its own platforms, scenes, defines, App ID, depots and branch.
  • Checks that catch release mistakes before players do: fog and shaders that vanish from the build, test builds, a version that was never bumped.
  • Upload with Valve's steamcmd from your own Steamworks SDK. Sign in once; the password is never stored.
  • Editor-only, full C# source, Unity 6 or newer, any render pipeline.
